## The pitch

> Rent-a-dog mould inspections: trained sniffer dogs check rentals for hidden
> mould before tenants move in — silly until you learn mould claims cost
> landlords billions a year.

Ridiculous at first glance: renting dogs to sniff houses. Real by the second:
dogs detect mould colonies behind walls far earlier than any visual survey,
and damp/mould disputes already cost landlords and insurers billions a year —
early detection is a genuine, billable service.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes the pitch to /output/entry.txt
```

Deterministic, no network, no input. The pitch is 173 characters (limit: 200).

Why dogs, not sensors: a trained nose finds a colony in minutes where a
moisture meter needs a wall opened first — speed is the product.
